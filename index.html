Master’s Thesis

Imperial College Business School · MSc Strategic Marketing

How can companies build future-proof brands?

An analysis of strategic foresight in the automotive industry

I looked at how brands survive disruption by comparing four automakers navigating two seismic shifts: electrification (Toyota vs. BYD) and autonomous driving (Waymo vs. Mercedes-Benz). The short version: speed alone doesn’t make a brand resilient. Toyota and Mercedes-Benz play it cautious, aligning innovation with brand values and scaling deliberately, while BYD and Waymo move fast and capture opportunity, at the cost of more reputational exposure. The brands that hold up best are the ones that embed foresight into both product strategy and brand narrative, not just one or the other.

That same approach carries into my GTM and commercial work: understand why a shift is happening before reacting to it, and build actionable strategies that have a long-lasting effect.
